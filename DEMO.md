# Demo script (about 90 seconds)

Live site: https://cliff-omega.vercel.app

**0:00 The idea (10s).** "Most OAuth guides rate the provider. The real question is: with these scopes, for these users, shipped this way, do I hit review? Gmail is ten minutes for your own inbox and six weeks plus a paid assessment for the public."

**0:10 A directory guide (30s).** Open Gmail from the directory.
- Header: difficulty, your hands-on time, approval wait. Every number has a dot. Click one: the exact quote, the URL, the fetch date.
- "Where's your cliff?": tick `gmail.readonly`. The card flips to "over the cliff" and a provider-owned wait appears on the critical path. Untick it: back to minutes.
- Scroll to "How we know": what the model assumed before research next to what the docs say, and the corrections.

**0:40 Live generation (40s).** Ask the graders for an app that is not in the directory and type it on the home page.
- Pip's stages are the real pipeline events: finding docs, reading and quoting, probing endpoints, double-checking, writing. Facts land in the ledger with their status as they are found. A fact that fails the re-check is downgraded on screen.
- Typical run: 60 to 90 seconds, about $0.50. The guide opens with a permanent share link and raw JSON.

**1:20 Honesty (10s).** Type nonsense, or an API-key-only service such as OpenWeatherMap. Nonsense gets an explicit "not a verified guide". An API-key service gets "No OAuth app" with the key path instead of an invented review process.

If a live run fails or the daily budget is spent, every directory guide still works, and "Re-verify sources" re-checks pages with no model cost.
